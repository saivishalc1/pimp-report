01 — Who we are

The P.I.M.P. Report

The Proven Insights on Market Predictability Report has a 20+ year history of accurately predicting future market movements and economic events up to many years in advance with a high level of precision. Our reports are better known as predictive market analyses, or PMAs.

All PMAs are dated upon completion. This is strategically important. PMAs can be rigorously compared to reports from well-respected financial news organizations such as Bloomberg, FT, CNBC and others on the same topic, with specific focus on the dates of those reports. Ultimately, those reports verify the content of the PMAs — because the PMAs always pre-date them.

Our mission is to give investors and traders leverage — a strategic advantage never before experienced.

Investors and traders are able to make more intelligent strategic investment decisions using the content of the PMAs. Due to ERC-721 technology (NFT), PMAs will now be structured as NFTs — making them digital assets that can be traded. Tradeable PMAs are the future of the DeFi revolution.

Our PMAs will be issued in limited series. There will be a limited number of PMAs minted for each series. Only a few will be able to obtain these PMAs, so only a few will be able to benefit from them. Become one of the few.
